# bed linen + extra sheets

## What

Duvet covers and fitted sheets — a couple of spare sets so there's always a clean one. Confirm mattress + duvet size first.

## Deniz's recommendation

[IKEA NATTJASMIN overtrek + ULLVIDE hoeslaken](https://www.ikea.com/nl/nl/cat/hoeslakens-10681/f/hoeslakens-140x200-si056/) — real percale at half HEMA's fitted-sheet price. Mix 1 NATTJASMIN set with 2 cheaper ÄNGSLILJA/DVALA spares so the rotation costs ~€150.

Confirm duvet size first — prices assume 140x200 (1-persoons); a 2-persoons duvet (200x200/240x220) shifts everything up 40–60%.
