# new glasses (eyewear)

## What

Proper pair of glasses — the fallback for days the [[contact-lenses|lenses]] stay in the case, so this outranks the lenses. Needs a current prescription (incl. pupillary distance) before ordering. Decide: high-street optician (Pearle / Hans Anders / Specsavers, fitting included) vs online (Ace & Tate, Charlie Temple), plus lens options — thin/high-index, anti-reflective, blue-light filter.

## Deniz's recommendation

[Specsavers](https://www.specsavers.nl/aanbiedingen/gratis-glasoptie) — cheapest route to thin + anti-reflective lenses with a real eye test and in-store fitting. 40% off all lenses until 30 Aug 2026. €130–165 band is an estimate from their offer pages — confirm in store. Budget €150 is the midpoint.
